Which console is in your scorer's box?

Every lesson, drill and the cheat sheet follow the console you pick. Look for the maker's name on the console itself.

Hockey timing in two minutes

Whistle = clock off

The game clock only runs while the puck is in play. Every time a referee blows the whistle, you flip the clock switch off.

Puck drop = clock on

Play restarts with a faceoff. The moment the referee drops the puck, flip the switch back on. Not when they skate to the dot, when the puck leaves their hand.

Penalty clocks follow the game clock

A penalty shows a player number and a countdown. It only counts down while the game clock runs, so you never have to start it separately.

Power-play goals end minors

If the team with more skaters scores, the shorthanded team's minor penalty ends right away. The console doesn't do this for you. You clear it.

Shots on goal

A shot that the goalie stops, or that goes in. Shots that miss the net or hit a post don't count. The clock keeps running; just tap the key.

The referee is the boss

If anything on the board is wrong, the ref tells you what it should be. Fixing a mistake is normal. Every lesson shows how.
